Privacy policy

The Daily Drop · Last updated 19 November 2026

The Daily Drop sets a new quote as your phone's wallpaper every day. It is built to work without knowing anything about you.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not read your contacts, photos, location or any other personal data.

What stays on your phone

Your settings, such as the categories, styles and update time you pick, are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Network requests

Once a day the app downloads a small public file with that day's quotes from GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com) or its mirror jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net). Like any web request, these services receive your device's IP address and basic request details. The app sends nothing else. See the GitHub privacy statement and the jsDelivr privacy policy.

Permissions

  • Internet, to download the daily quote file
  • Set wallpaper, to update your lock and home screen

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to the developer email listed on the app's Google Play page.
